How It's Built

Infrastructure Proposal

The system is designed to integrate with Kandy's existing urban fabric with minimum disruption.

🏗️
Elevated Tubes
Transparent or semi-opaque tubes mounted on reinforced columns above road medians and edges.
🏛️
Support Columns
Pylon-style columns spaced along road shoulders — no private land acquisition where roads exist.
🛗
Vertical Lift Stations
Elevator and stairway access from ground level. Stations attached to buildings via skybridges.
🚨
Emergency Exits
Safe exit platforms every defined interval. Manual evacuation walkways alongside main tube.
☀️
Solar Canopy
Solar panels integrated on tube tops and station roofs. Battery backup nodes at every station.
📡
Smart Sensors
Vibration, heat, structural load, crowd flow, pod location and emergency detection sensors.
🔇
Noise Reduction Shell
Acoustic dampening on tube exterior. Pod speed limited in dense residential zones.
🌧️
Weather Protection
Lightning arrestors, drainage channels, flood-resistant station design, rain sensor override.
🔧
Maintenance Rail
Inspection drone rail and hatches alongside tubes. Scheduled maintenance windows at night.
Risk Awareness

Safety & Hazard Planning

This concept takes real-world constraints seriously. Every design choice accounts for Kandy's unique geography, heritage, and urban density.

🏛️
Heritage Zones
No elevated structures within restricted zones near the Temple of the Tooth. Routes re-planned around cultural corridors.
🏔️
Hilly Terrain
Seismic and vibration monitoring for all columns. Soil stability assessment before any pylon placement.
🌊
Flood & Landslide
Route planning avoids known landslide-prone corridors. Flood sensors trigger automatic pod hold.
🔥
Fire-Safe Stations
Non-combustible materials, fire suppression systems, clear evacuation signage and emergency lighting.
🚪
Evacuation Platforms
Rescue platforms accessible every 150m of tube. Manual access doors for emergency crew.
🏘️
Right-of-Way
Routes use public road corridors first. Private land only with compensation and legal approval.
⚡
Lightning Arrest
Full lightning rod network across tube spans. Grounding systems at every column base.
🤖
Inspection Drones
Automated structural inspection drones run nightly checks. Alerts sent to control center.

Honest Assessment

Benefits & Challenges

✅ POTENTIAL BENEFITS
  • Reduced road congestion in city center
  • Faster connection between all city zones
  • Emergency priority routes save lives
  • Cleaner, quieter city movement
  • Better integration with rail & bus
  • Tourism-friendly routes near landmarks
  • Less pressure on narrow Kandy roads
  • Real-time city data and smart routing
  • Renewable energy powered operation
  • Barrier-free access with lifts and ramps
⚠️ REAL CHALLENGES
  • Extremely high construction cost
  • Complex regulatory and government approvals
  • Land and right-of-way legal issues
  • Ongoing maintenance complexity
  • Safety certification requirements
  • Heritage and environmental restrictions
  • Evacuation planning in dense city
  • Public acceptance and trust
  • Engineering challenges in hilly terrain
  • Seismic resilience design requirements
