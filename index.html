How It's Built

Infrastructure Design

🏗️
Elevated Loop Tubes
Transparent sealed tubes above road medians on reinforced columns.
🏛️
Pylon Columns
Placed along road shoulders — no private land required where public roads exist.
🛗
Pod Stations
Elevator + stair access. Skybridges to adjacent buildings. Ground drop-off zones.
📱
AJRLoops App
Book your pod, select destination, see live ETA. Board with QR code.
🔀
Smart Junctions
AI-controlled switching nodes where loops meet. Route selection in milliseconds.
☀️
Solar Canopy
Solar panels on tube tops and station roofs. Battery backup at every node.
🚨
Emergency Exits
Rescue platforms every 150m. Manual access doors for emergency crew.
🔧
Night Maintenance
Inspection drones launch after midnight. Scheduled hatches on tube exterior.
📡
Sensor Network
Vibration, heat, structural load, crowd flow and emergency detection.

Honest Assessment

Benefits & Challenges

✅ POTENTIAL BENEFITS
  • Reduced road congestion city-wide
  • Faster connection between all zones
  • Emergency priority routes save lives
  • Cleaner, quieter city movement
  • App-based booking — no queue
  • Tourism-friendly cultural routes
  • Real-time AI scheduling and data
  • Solar-powered, low-carbon operation
⚠️ REAL CHALLENGES
  • Extremely high construction cost
  • Complex regulatory approvals
  • Land and right-of-way issues
  • Safety certification requirements
  • Heritage and environmental restrictions
  • Engineering challenges in hilly terrain
  • Public acceptance and trust
  • Seismic resilience design required
