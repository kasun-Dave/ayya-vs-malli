How It's Built

Infrastructure Design

Every element designed to sit above Kandy's streets with minimum disruption to existing life.

🏗️
Elevated Loop Tubes
Transparent or semi-opaque sealed tubes on reinforced columns above road medians.
🏛️
Pylon Columns
Placed along road shoulders — no private land acquisition where public roads exist.
🛗
Pod Stations
Elevator + stair access. Skybridges to adjacent buildings. Ground-level drop-off zones.
📱
AJRLoops App
Book your pod, select destination, see live ETA, board with QR code. Real-time tracking.
🔀
Smart Junctions
AI-controlled switching nodes where loops intersect. Pods route to correct branch in milliseconds.
☀️
Solar Canopy
Solar panels on tube tops and station roofs. Battery backup at every node.
📡
Sensor Network
Vibration, heat, structural load, crowd flow, pod location and emergency detection.
🚨
Emergency Exits
Rescue platforms every 150m. Manual access doors. Evacuation walkways alongside tubes.
🔧
Night Maintenance
Inspection drones launch after midnight. Hatches on tube exterior for manual access.
Risk Awareness

Safety & Hazard Planning

Real constraints. Real terrain. Kandy's unique geography demands careful, honest engineering.

🏛️
Heritage Zones
No elevated structures near Temple of the Tooth. Routes re-planned around cultural corridors.
🏔️
Hilly Terrain
Seismic and vibration monitoring. Soil stability assessment before any pylon placement.
🌊
Flood & Landslide
Routes avoid known landslide corridors. Flood sensors trigger automatic pod hold.
🔥
Fire-Safe Stations
Non-combustible materials, fire suppression, clear evacuation signage and emergency lighting.
🚪
Evacuation Platforms
Rescue platforms every 150m. Manual access doors for emergency crew entry.
⚡
Lightning Protection
Full lightning rod network across tube spans. Grounding systems at every column base.

Honest Assessment

Benefits & Challenges

✅ POTENTIAL BENEFITS
  • Reduced road congestion city-wide
  • Faster connection between all zones
  • Emergency priority routes save lives
  • Cleaner, quieter city movement
  • App-based booking — no queue, no wait
  • Tourism-friendly cultural routes
  • Less pressure on narrow Kandy roads
  • Real-time AI scheduling and data
  • Solar-powered, low-carbon operation
  • Barrier-free access with lifts
⚠️ REAL CHALLENGES
  • Extremely high construction cost
  • Complex regulatory approvals
  • Land and right-of-way legal issues
  • Ongoing maintenance complexity
  • Safety certification requirements
  • Heritage and environmental restrictions
  • Evacuation planning in dense city
  • Public acceptance and trust
  • Engineering challenges in hilly terrain
  • Seismic resilience design required
